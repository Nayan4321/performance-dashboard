# Salon Call Center Quality Audit Matrix

How your calls are audited and scored. Every call is checked on 5 parameters, scored 1 to 5, and some mistakes fail the whole call.

## 1. Hospitality & Energy
- Warm salon greeting.
- Sound enthusiastic and welcoming.
- Use the caller's name.
- Represent a luxury, friendly salon vibe.

## 2. Booking Accuracy
- Select the correct **stylist, service type, date and branch** in the software.
- Spell the client's details correctly.
- Capture phone and email.

## 3. Consultation & Upsell
- Ask discovery questions (e.g. hair length, specific goals).
- Proactively suggest add-ons (treatments, manicures) or premium stylists.

## 4. Policy Disclosure
! Write the client's needs and requirements in Zenoti as notes for the stylist.
- Clearly state requirements and needs (for example for hair colour) or arrival times.
- Record them on the Zenoti CRM.

## 5. Closing & Recap
- Repeat the final appointment details: **Date, Time, Stylist, Service, Price**.
- Thank the client warmly.

## Auditing by call length
**0 to 1 min** (cancellations, reschedules, directions, new booking)
- Empathy during cancellations.
- Smooth rescheduling without losing the client.
- Speed of giving directions, basic pricing or new bookings.

**1 to 3 min** (standard bookings, single services)
- Natural upselling: did you offer a conditioning treatment with that haircut?
- Basic booking accuracy.

**3+ min** (complex bookings, complaints, consultations)
- Patience and problem-solving.
- Usually multi-service bookings (e.g. Colour + Cut + Blow-dry), bridal inquiries or unhappy clients.
- Did you fill the salon calendar gaps efficiently?

## Scoring rubric (1 to 5)
- **5 Excellent:** exceeds expectations. Natural flow, flawless system entry, proactive upselling, enthusiastic tone.
- **4 Good:** meets all standards. Clear communication and accurate booking, but no proactive or enthusiastic edge.
- **3 Average:** missed minor steps (e.g. forgot the client's name) or flat tone, but completed the core request.
- **2 Below average:** critical gaps. No policies mentioned, wrong service type entered, or sounded scripted or bored.
- **1 Poor:** argumentative tone, incorrect pricing, or wrong date/time booked in the system.

## Auto-fail: the call scores 0 / 25
! Any one of these makes the whole call zero, whatever the other scores.
- **Booking disasters:** wrong date, time, location or stylist booked without the client's consent.
- **Zero policy disclosure:** the client's needs, requests and requirements not added in the CRM as notes for the stylist.
- **Hostile behaviour:** rudeness, talking over the client, or hanging up early.
- **Data breach:** credit card or personal contact details left unverified or unsecured, or client info handled through personal numbers.

## Text confirmation
! Say this on every booking.
> "I have confirmed your mobile number as [Number]. You will receive a text confirmation in the next two minutes with your appointment details and our location link."

**Why it matters:** it reduces no-shows and makes sure the client's contact details in the software are live and accurate. Team Leads audit this line.
