# Grand Flora Luxury Customer Service & Complaint Handling Protocol

Complaint handling, outbound call scripts and team accountability for Dubai's luxury market. Prepared by Jeet Kalra.

## Deeply Respectful Greeting (Acknowledge the Relationship)
Process step – Greet them as family to make them feel special.
1. The Greeting: "Welcome back to Grand Flora, Ya Ahla W Sahla. I see your name on our priority file; it is a true honor to speak with you today."
2. Empathy Statement: "I hear you are upset, and we are not happy if you are not. You are a valued part of the Grand Flora family; your satisfaction is our priority."
3. Invitation: "Please, tell me exactly what happened so I can personally take care of this for you."
4. Quick Action: Look up their history immediately. If they have been a client for years, mention it: "You have been with us for 2 years, we care about your loyalty."

## High-Respect Fact-Checking (Branch Collaboration)
Coordinate for elite service standards.
1. The Frame: Frame the fact-checking not as an "investigation," but as a high-standard coordination to give them the elite service they deserve.
2. The Script: "[Client Name], our reputation with you means everything to us. To make sure I resolve this perfectly and with the highest standards, please allow me to contact the Branch Manager at [Branch Name] right now."
3. The Intent: "I want to personally check their service logs so I can understand exactly what happened during your session and find the most beautiful solution for you."

## The Luxury Hold
Keep interactions warm and grateful.
1. The Request: "May I place you on a brief, respectful hold for just two minutes?"
2. The Alternative: Or ask for a callback: "I'll call the manager directly on her mobile to get this sorted for you immediately."
3. The Appreciation: "I truly appreciate your patience with us."

## Delivering the Resolution with Hospitality
Process step – Presenting solutions as gestures of deep respect.
1. The Appreciation: "Thank you for your gracious patience, [Client Name]. I spoke with the team. Because you are valuable, we want to ensure you leave us completely happy."
2. The Category B Delivery: "I have arranged a private, priority appointment. Our Senior Stylist is expecting you and will personally perfect everything to your exact liking."
3. The Added Value: "I have also added a VIP note to your profile so the entire team knows exactly how to pamper you on your next visit. We want you to always feel at home with us."
4. The Closing Question: "Shall I lock in this special timing for you?"

## Cultural Nuances for Agents
Softening the tone through prestigious etiquette.
1. Use Warm Honorifics: Freely use respectful terms like "Habibti" (if appropriate/female agent), "Ya Ahla W Sahla", or "Ya Hala". It softens the tone instantly.
2. The Power of "Ghalia": Reminding them that they are a Ghalia (highly valued/precious) client makes them feel respected and tones down aggression.
3. Firm but Soft Limits: For unreasonable demands on Category A/B, say: "While we cannot change the bill for this specific session, I am personally ensuring your next experience is upgraded to the absolute highest VIP standard."

## Emirati & Arab Hospitality Trainee Rules
Tuning into Dubai luxury market expectations.
- Elegant Greetings: Start with a warm "Assalamu Alaikum" or a highly respectful "Good morning/evening, Madam [Name]".
- Emphasize Convenience: Remind them we have 6 beautiful locations across Dubai. They can book at whichever branch is closest to their gatherings or home.
- Leverage Our 30-Year Heritage: Frame the call as a premium check-in from their trusted beauty family.
- The "No-Hard-Sell" Rule: Focus entirely on pampering them, reserving favorite therapists, and offering peace of mind.

## Scenario 1: The VIP Regular
Targeting visits every 15 days.
1. The Pitch: "Assalamu Alaikum, Madam [Name]! This is [Your Name] from Grand Flora. Your regular 15-day refresh is coming up next week."
2. The Scarcity: "Your favorite specialist, [Name], specifically asked me to call you because her weekend slots are filling up quickly."
3. The Flexibility: "Should we book your usual time at our [mention current branch location], or would one of our other 5 branches suit you better?"

## Scenario 2: The Steady Monthly Visitor
Aligning with monthly self-care routines.
1. The Pitch: "Good morning/evening, Madam [Name]. This is [Your Name] from Grand Flora Salon. It's been a month since we last welcomed you for your [mention service, e.g., signature hair ritual/Moroccan bath]."
2. Expertise Framing: "As you know, keeping up that beautiful glow takes just that monthly touch, and after 30 years of looking after the city's finest ladies, we know exactly how to keep your hair/nails looking perfect."
3. The Reserve: "I have premium morning openings this Thursday, or a relaxing afternoon slot on Saturday. Which one may I reserve for your self-care time?"

## Scenario 3: The Bi-Monthly Visitor
1. The Pitch: "Assalamu Alaikum, Dear Madam [Name]. This is [Your Name] from Grand Flora. We have missed your presence in the salon so much! How have you been? It has been a couple of months since your last visit."
2. Seasonal Needs: "Because Grand Flora has such a vast variety of luxury treatments, from our exclusive hair spas to our traditional baths, we always love to see what your skin and hair need next as the seasons change."
3. The Invitation: "I wanted to personally invite you back to any of our 6 locations across Dubai. What day this coming week can we block out just for you to step away, relax, and be completely pampered?"

## Scenario 4: The Lapsed Customer
Restoring relationships with deep respect.
1. The Pitch: "Assalamu Alaikum, Madam [Name]. I hope you and your family are doing beautifully. This is [Your Name] from Grand Flora. As a leading Dubai salon for 30+ years, our guests are family to us."
2. The Gentle Absence: "While reviewing our guest books, we noticed your gentle absence—it has been over three months since we last looked after you, and we truly missed your visits."
3. The Open Door: "I wanted to reach out personally to ensure you are well. Whenever you are ready to return, our doors across all 6 locations are open. May we have the honor of welcoming you back?"

## Objection: "I am busy, call me later."
Respecting time with high-respect blessings.
1. Trainee Strategy: Never just say "okay, bye." Instantly acknowledge her busy schedule with respect, offer a quick alternative, or set a specific time to reconnect.
2. The Tactic: "Of course, Madam [Name]! Your time is precious and I completely understand. May Allah give you strength (Yateeki Al Afiah). I will not take another moment of your day."
3. The Next Step: "Would it be better if I sent you a quick WhatsApp with our latest premium slots across our 6 branches, or may I call you back tomorrow afternoon around this same time when you are more relaxed?"

## Objection: "Service/Therapist Issues"
! Never defend the salon or argue. Apologise instantly.
Using our 30-year legacy as a shield.
1. Trainee Strategy: Never defend the salon or argue. Apologize instantly and sincerely. Position the return visit as a way for us to make it right.
2. The Tactic: "Oh, Madam [Name], I am deeply sorry. Please accept my apologies. For 30 years, Grand Flora has been a market leader because we treat our ladies like royalty; hearing you left less than delighted breaks my heart."
3. Resolution Strategy: "I am personally sharing your feedback with our Area Salon Manager right now. I want to personally handpick our top master specialist at [preferred branch] to look after you next time. May I look at our calendar?"

## Competition Offer or Lower Price
! Never lower prices or give discounts over the phone.
Shifting focus from price to heritage and safety.
1. Trainee Strategy: Never lower prices or discount over the phone. Remind her politely that cheap offers often come with compromised quality.
2. The Tactic: "I understand, Madam [Name]. Many new salons have discounts. However, your hair, skin, and well-being deserve ultimate peace of mind."
3. Value Proposition: "For over 30 years, Dubai's ladies have trusted Grand Flora. We never compromise on premium products, medical-grade hygiene, and elite, certified specialists."

## When She Goes Ahead
Closing the loop with warm blessings.
- VIP/Monthly Closing: "Perfect, Habibti! I have securely locked in your signature slot for [Day] at [Time] with [Stylist Name] at our [Branch Name] location. I will send a confirmation to your WhatsApp right now with the location map. Have a beautiful day, Madam!"
- Bi-Monthly/Lapsed Closing: "It is truly our honor, Madam [Name]! We are so delighted to welcome you back into the Grand Flora family. I am sending your VIP confirmation details to your WhatsApp immediately. Look forward to seeing you, and may you have a blessed week ahead!"

## Bi-Weekly Strategic KPI Dashboard
Evaluating system health and operational bottlenecks.
- Systemic Bottleneck Tracker: Frequency of recurring errors per branch. GM uses data to adjust scheduling capacity or management.
- Customer Retention Rate (CRR): Formula = Complaining customers saved and rebooked / total complaining customers logged × 100. Measures financial effectiveness of recovery.
- Public Brand Reputation Velocity: Net change in Google Business and Social Media scores across all 6 locations.
- Booking Accuracy & Staff Optimization: Number of successful, un-disputed bookings versus total slots filled. Identifies communication friction.

## Financial Penalties & Incentive Policy
Directly tying performance metrics to rewards.
- Incentive Pool Payout: Target Met criteria: 100% Ticket Resolution, 4.8+ Star Rating Average, and Zero Unexcused Drops.
- Progressive Penalty Tier: Target Missed stages:
  1. Tier 1 (Verbal Warning)
  2. Tier 2 (Deduction Points)
  3. Tier 3 (Commission Impact)

## Staff Accountability Tiers
! Branch heads must resolve open tickets within 24 hours.
Roles, penalties, and resolution requirements.
1. Call Center Staff: High volume/low error incentives. Penalties: 1st Typo (Warning), 2nd Typo (Bonus deduction), 3rd Typo (Salary deduction of comp).
2. Branch Floor Staff: If poor styling/rude treatment causes a negative review, comp costs are charged against the guilty stylist's commission pool.
3. Branch Head Accountability: Failure to resolve an open ticket within the mandatory 24-hour window leads to a penalty fee from store-performance bonuses.
4. Speed of Resolution: Complaints must be solved based on the provided discount/FOC annexure to ensure fast responses.
