# Grand Flora Salon Master Inbound Script

How to handle every inbound call, from the first greeting to the 30-minute pre-arrival call. Prepared by Jeet Kalra.

## Stage 1: The Welcome & Service Choice
Setting the tone of luxury and dedicated service from the very first moment of contact.
1. Greeting: "Thank you for calling Grand Flora Salon!"
2. Self-Introduction: "This is [Agent Name], your dedicated beauty coordinator."
3. Booking Inquiry: "Are we booking your pampering session at our luxurious [Branch Location]?"
4. Alternative Offer: "Would you prefer our premium Luxury Home Service today?"

## Situation 1: The 15-Day Regular (Loyal & Routine-Driven)
Capitalizing on the meticulous nature of loyal guests with targeted hair-health upgrades.
- Future Bookings:
  - Confirm regular slot
  - Offer treatment
- Same-Day Bookings:
  - Build rapport
  - Confirm booking

## Situation 2: The Once-a-Month Customer
Rewarding consistent monthly guests with exclusive seasonal refresh bundles and discounts.
- Next-Day or Later Booking:
  - Confirm visit for usual schedule
  - Upsell Signature Mani-Pedi bundle
- Same-Day Booking:
  - Prioritize rapport building
  - Upsell during confirmation

## Situation 3: The 2-Month Deal-Chaser Customer (Price-Sensitive)
Using high-value bonuses and "welcome back" incentives to re-engage price-conscious clients.
- Option A: Future Date
  - Confirm visit time
  - Offer booking bonus
- Option B: Same-Day Booking
  - Build warm rapport
  - Book reset session

## Stage 3: The Mandatory Re-Confirmation Block (Part 1)
! Mandatory on every booking: re-confirm before ending the call.
Finalizing the initial booking with a focus on personalization, safety, and VIP care.
1. The Script: "Perfect, [Client Name]! Let me officially lock in your flawless Grand Flora experience."
2. Appointment: "I have you secured for [Day/Date] at exactly [Time] with your stylist, [Stylist Name]."
3. Your Setup: "We are prepping our [Branch Salon Station / Luxury Home Kit] for your [Main Service]."
4. Safety: "Tools sanitized before you arrive (or team brings medical-grade sterilized kits)."
5. VIP Touch: "Personal notes added to your file. A confirmation link is heading to your phone now."

## Stage 3: The 30-Minute Pre-Arrival Call (Part 2)
! Call every client 30 minutes before arrival.
A high-energy check-in call designed to trigger high-conversion flash upgrades right before arrival.
1. The Introduction: "Hi [Client Name], this is [Your Name] from Grand Flora. We are so excited to see you in about 30 minutes! Your stylist, [Stylist Name], is already setting up."
2. The Courtesy Update: "We just wanted to confirm we are fully ready for your arrival at [Time]." (OR for Home Service: "Our team is on their way and will arrive right at [Time].")
3. Same-Day Flash Upgrade: "Since we are preparing your station, we have a same-day flash upgrade available: our premium hydrating hair mask for half-price today only."
4. Final Check: "Perfect! Drive safely, and we will have [mention personal touch, e.g., your favorite tea / your VIP room] waiting for you. See you very soon!"

## How the New Process Works
Understanding the strategic shift from initial setup to high-conversion revenue generation.
- The Initial Call (Setup Phase): Focuses on reassurance, accuracy, and building luxury value. You collect their personal preference (tea, temperature, music) here to use as your relationship-building tool.
- The 30-Minute Call (Action & Revenue Phase): Acts as a traffic check and high-conversion sales window. Pitching a half-price upgrade as the stylist "preps their station" makes the offer feel exclusive, immediate, and high-value.

## Objection Handling and Strategic Value Pivots
Standardized responses to common customer concerns to ensure luxury positioning and smooth closes.
1. The "How much is it?" – Pivot to value: "It's usually [Full Price], but today only [Half Price]. Adds no time, locks in shine."
2. The "I'm in a rush" – Reassure: "[Stylist Name] applies it during backwash; won't add a minute to your departure time."
3. The "Maybe next time" – Maintain premium image: "No worries, [Client Name]! We'll keep your original plan exactly as is."
4. The "My hair is fine" – Expert opinion: "[Stylist Name] will check your hair. If it benefits, we'll honor the discount."

## 3 Golden Rules for Agents Handling (Best Practices)
! Never argue with a client.
Essential behavioral standards to ensure professional and effective client interactions.
1. Never Argue: Accept the objection instantly with "I completely understand" or "Of course!" before offering a solution.
2. Use the Stylist's Name: Bringing the stylist into the conversation makes the recommendation feel customized and professional, not corporate.
3. Keep it Short: The client is 30 minutes away from their appointment—likely driving or finishing work. Keep the interaction brisk and cheerful.
