# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Matrix & Chain of Command

Complaint response times, who owns each step, and when to escalate. Presented by Jeet.

1. Ms. Kawther logs the ticket and initiates tracking
2. Group alert sent to notify relevant support team
3. First call by Ms. Nida to address the issue
4. Second call by Mr. Saif for further escalation
5. Ticket closure by Ms. Kawther after resolution

Standard Ticket Timeline (75 Minutes):
1. Ms. Kawther logs the ticket and records incident details

3. First call by Ms. Nida to follow up with the customer
4. Second call by Mr. Saif for escalated handling

## VIP Workflow (45-Minute Target)
! VIP complaints must be resolved and closed within 45 minutes.
1. Step 1 – Logging & Routing (0:00–5:00)
   - Complaint logged in system with VIP priority tag
   - Formal ticket created for tracking and follow-up
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Kawther (Frontline Team Lead)
   - Ensures immediate visibility and correct routing of issue
2. Step 2 – Group Broadcast (5:00–15:00)
   - Detailed complaint ticket shared in Outlet Heads WhatsApp group
   - All outlet leaders receive case context and urgency
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Kawther (Frontline Team Lead)
   - Enables rapid coordination and initial action at outlet level
3. Step 3 – First Operations Escalation (15:00–35:00)
   - Direct phone call to Outlet Head if no digital acknowledgment within 10 minutes of group alert
   - Confirms ownership and action plan for the VIP case
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Nida (Head of Frontline & Operations Liaison)
   - Reduces response risk through personal escalation and alignment
4. Step 4 – Final Resolution Call (35:00–45:00)
   - Urgent escalation call to outlet exactly 10 minutes before SLA breach
   - Objective: secure definitive resolution before 45-minute deadline
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Nida (Head of Frontline & Operations Liaison)
   - Locks in solution path to protect VIP experience and SLA compliance
5. Step 5 – Client Closure (by 45:00)
   - Resolution communicated clearly and seamlessly to VIP client
   - Complaint ticket formally closed in the system
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Kawther (Frontline Team Lead)
   - Confirms client satisfaction and documents final outcome

## Standard Workflow (75-Minute Target)
! Standard complaints must be resolved and closed within 75 minutes.
Tier 2 Overview & Time Milestones:
- Tier 2: Standard Client Workflow with a 75-minute resolution target
- Total duration: 1 hour 15 minutes from complaint logging to client closure
- Key control points at: 00:00, 10:00, 20:00, 65:00, and 75:00 minutes
- Clear ownership assigned at each stage to ensure accountability

Milestones:
1. 00:00 – Logging & Routing
   - Complaint registered in the system with a Standard priority tag
   - Ticket created and categorized for Tier 2 handling
   - Initial routing to appropriate service channel based on complaint type
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Kawther (Frontline Team Lead)
2. 10:00 – Group Broadcast to Outlet Heads
   - Ticket details broadcasted to Outlet Heads via group chat
   - Ensures rapid awareness across all outlet leadership
   - Digital trail created for monitoring acknowledgment and response time
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Kawther (Frontline Team Lead)
3. 20:00 – First Operations Escalation
   - Direct phone call to Outlet Head if no digital acknowledgment received
   - Triggered 10 minutes after the group alert to avoid silent delays
   - Escalation focuses on confirming responsibility and next actions
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Nida (Head of Frontline & Operations Liaison)
4. 65:00 – Final Resolution Escalation
   - Urgent escalation call initiated 10 minutes before SLA breach
   - Objective: lock in a concrete resolution and confirm implementation
   - Used as a last operational intervention before the SLA deadline
   - Primary ownership: Mr. Saif (Operations & Escalation Director)
5. 75:00 – Client Closure & Ticket Completion
   - Resolution communicated and delivered to the client
   - Client updated on actions taken and final outcome of the complaint
   - Ticket formally closed in the system and marked as resolved
   - Primary ownership: Ms. Kawther (Frontline Team Lead)

## Core Operational Rules
- Frontline Gatekeeper:
  - Ms. Kawther captures every client complaint immediately and without exception
  - Starts and monitors the overall countdown timer for each complaint case
  - Ensures no complaint is lost, overlooked, or delayed at intake
  - Acts as the primary control point for complaint logging and time tracking
- Operations Liaison:
  - Ms. Nida connects Frontline with the wider branch and outlet network
  - Uses formal authority to push Outlet Heads for instant digital acknowledgement
  - Ensures swift registration of complaints in all relevant systems
  - Minimizes handover delays between Frontline and operational outlets
- Final Escalation Authority:
  - Mr. Saif or Ms. Nida intervenes strictly at the final escalation milestone
  - Removes internal friction and decision bottlenecks to protect client timelines
  - Enforces a binding resolution before any delay reaches the client
  - Provides ultimate escalation path to secure SLA compliance and closure

## Branch Penalty & Acknowledgment
1. Branch Penalty Matrix for SLA Breaches:
   - A structured penalty matrix applies to all branches for SLA violations
   - Any missed milestone automatically triggers administrative sanctions
   - Penalties are designed to enforce strict operational accountability
   - Application of the matrix is uniform across all outlets, with no exceptions
2. Digital Acknowledgment Criteria:
   - Alerts are deemed legally "Acknowledged" only under explicit conditions
   - The Outlet Head must respond directly in the designated group chat
   - Response must be specific, actionable, and related to the alert content
   - Generic or silent receipt of the alert does not meet acknowledgment standards

## Response Rules
! "Checking" with no time promise, or an emoji-only reply, is not an acknowledgment.
Triggers call (not accepted):
- Unacceptable replies: "Checking" without any clear or specific time promise is not acceptable and will directly trigger Ms. Nida's follow-up call.
- Missing timelines: When a timeline is promised but the person gives no follow-up or status update, the lack of response will be treated as unacceptable.
- Emoji-only replies: Any reply that is only an emoji, such as a thumbs up sent by the branch, is not accepted as a valid or trackable response.

Stops call (accepted):
- Simple acknowledgments: Short text acknowledgments like "Ok" or "Noted" are accepted as valid responses when they clearly show receipt of the request.
- Action with ETA: Replies like "Received. Actioning now with team. ETA for fix: 15 mins." are valid because they confirm action and provide a clear timeline.
- Assignment updates: Responses such as "Confirmed receipt. Ticket assigned to supervisor [Name]. Resolution in progress" are accepted as they show ownership.

## Standard Client Resolution Script
Purpose of the Resolution Script:
- Provide a consistent framework for communicating resolutions to clients
- Ensure messaging aligns with the Service Level Agreement (SLA) Matrix
- Support clear escalation and chain-of-command communication
- Reduce ambiguity in next steps and ownership

Core Components of the Script:
1. Opening acknowledgment of issue and impact on the client
2. Brief summary of the problem as understood and investigated
3. Clear statement of the resolution provided or planned
4. Expected timelines based on SLA commitments
5. Defined responsibilities and contact points along the chain of command
6. Confirmation of next steps and how the client can follow up
